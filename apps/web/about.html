A Community Initiative for Public Good

This voter search tool was created with a simple objective: to make it easier for people to find and access information from the 2002 Karnataka Electoral Rolls.

Many citizens, researchers, and families often face challenges when searching through old voter lists. The information exists, but it is usually locked away in scanned documents that are difficult to search and navigate. We believed that technology could help bridge this gap and make this public information more accessible to everyone.

This initiative was developed by a group of engineers who studied in Mysore and have spent between 7 and 20 years working across the IT and Mechanical industries. Drawing upon our professional experience and technical skills, we came together to build a simple, user-friendly platform that can serve the community.

Free for Everyone

This platform has been built as a community service and is provided completely free of cost. There is no commercial or monetary intention behind this initiative.

Our goal is simply to help citizens, families, researchers, and anyone interested in accessing historical voter information in a convenient and searchable format.

Why We Built This

We strongly believe that technical skills can be used for purposes beyond commercial projects. Technology has the power to solve everyday problems, preserve valuable historical records, and make public information easier to access.

This project is our small contribution towards that larger goal.

Looking Ahead

We hope this tool makes it easier for people to reconnect with historical records, conduct research, and locate information that would otherwise require significant manual effort. As technology professionals, we see this as an opportunity to use our knowledge and experience for the broader benefit of society.

Thank you for using the platform and supporting community-driven initiatives.
