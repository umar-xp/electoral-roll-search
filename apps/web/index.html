Quick Guide

📌 Note: Few districts are added in the full search. For other districts, use the following 3 steps.
1
Visit voters.eci.gov.in and look for the most likely polling station or part number where your name may exist.
2
Go to ceo.karnataka.gov.in/voter_list/en and download the PDF(s) for your AC and part number.
3
Upload the PDF(s) below and enter your name. The tool will OCR and search the roll for you.
